Privacy Policy

Repact is a fitness challenge app that uses on-device camera processing to count exercise repetitions. This policy explains what information we process and the choices you have.

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Information we process

  • Account information: your email address, authentication provider identifier, and the profile name you enter.
  • Challenge and activity information: challenges, daily goals, repetition totals, streaks, participation, follows, reactions, and activity shared inside Repact.
  • Support information: the description, device context, and optional screenshot you choose to include when submitting a bug report.
  • Usage and technical information: app interactions, device/browser type, approximate region derived by our analytics providers, and diagnostic events.
  • Information stored only on your device: body measurements, before/after photos, recorded workout videos, and other local preferences, unless you explicitly choose to share or attach an item.

Camera processing

Live camera frames used for repetition counting are processed on your device and are not uploaded to Repact servers. Repact does not use camera frames to train AI models. A photo, video, or screenshot leaves your device only when you explicitly choose to share or submit it.

How we use information

  • Provide authentication, synced progress, challenges, and social features.
  • Count and verify workout progress on your device.
  • Maintain app security, diagnose failures, and respond to support requests.
  • Understand aggregate product usage and improve Repact.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Repact uses Google Firebase for authentication, database hosting, and analytics; PostHog for product analytics; Meta for Facebook Login; Google for Google Sign-In; and GitHub Pages for web hosting. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies as needed to deliver their services.

Retention and security

Account and synced challenge information is retained while your Repact account is active or until you request deletion. Local information remains on your device until you reset the app, clear browser data, or uninstall it. Analytics and security records may be retained for limited periods under provider settings or where reasonably necessary for security and legal obligations.

We use access controls and encrypted HTTPS connections, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can use Repact as a guest without connecting Facebook or Google.
  • You can revoke Repact’s Facebook access from Facebook’s Apps and Websites settings.
  • You can clear local test data from Repact’s Privacy & data screen.
  • You can request deletion of your account and server-side data at any time.

Children and changes
