Repact Privacy Policy

Your data

Delete your Repact data

You can request deletion of your Repact account and the server-side information associated with it. Removing Repact from Facebook alone revokes access but may not delete information already stored by Repact.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Request account deletion

  1. Open Profile → Account in Repact.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm the warning.
  3. If you cannot access the app, email repactapp@gmail.com from the address connected to your account and use the subject Repact data deletion request.
Email deletion request

What happens next

We may ask you to confirm account ownership. After verification, we will delete or anonymize the personal account information, synced workout progress, challenge participation, follows, reactions, activity, and support submissions associated with your account. Shared challenge records may retain non-identifying aggregate totals so other participants’ challenge history remains consistent.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Remove local data

Photos, measurements, recorded workout videos, and some preferences are stored on your device. To remove them, use Profile → Privacy & data → Reset all test data, clear the site’s browser data, or uninstall Repact. The in-app Delete account action removes the associated server-side account data.

Remove Facebook access

In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select Repact, and choose Remove. This prevents future Facebook access. To delete information already stored by Repact, also submit the deletion request above.

Questions

Contact repactapp@gmail.com or read the Repact Privacy Policy.
